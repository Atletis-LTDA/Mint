Every registration you complete on Atletis is stored in **Meus Ingressos** (My Tickets) — your personal registration hub on the platform. Whether you want to print your confirmation, check whether your boleto payment has cleared, or look up the start time for an upcoming event, Meus Ingressos is the place to go. You don't need to keep track of confirmation emails; everything is accessible here as long as you're logged in.

## Accessing Meus Ingressos

<Steps>
  <Step title="Go to atletis.com.br">
    Open [atletis.com.br](https://www.atletis.com.br) in your browser on desktop or mobile.
  </Step>

  <Step title="Log In to Your Account">
    Click the **login** link and sign in with the email address and password you used when you created your Atletis account. If you registered for an event as a guest and don't yet have an account, you can create one using the same email address you used at checkout.
  </Step>

  <Step title="Open Meus Ingressos">
    Once logged in, navigate to **Meus Ingressos** (My Tickets) from your account menu. All your past and upcoming registrations are listed here.
  </Step>

  <Step title="Select a Registration">
    Click on any registration to see its full details, including payment status, QR code, event information, and any updates posted by the organizer.
  </Step>
</Steps>

## What You Can See in Meus Ingressos

Each registration entry in Meus Ingressos displays:

- **Event name** and the organizer's event page link
- **Event date and location** (city and state)
- **Your category** (the age group, gender, or distance you registered under)
- **Payment status** — confirmed, pending, or failed (see below)
- **QR code** — available once payment is confirmed; use this for check-in on event day where applicable
- **Registration confirmation details** — for printing or downloading

## Printing or Downloading Your Confirmation

Your registration confirmation is available to print or download directly from Meus Ingressos as soon as your payment is confirmed. Open the relevant registration entry and look for the print or download option. Your confirmation is also attached to the confirmation email sent to your registered email address.

## Understanding Payment Status

| Status | What it means |
|---|---|
| **Confirmed** | Your payment has been received and processed. You're officially registered. |
| **Pending** | Your payment is being processed. This is normal for boleto payments while the bank processes the slip (up to 3 business days). |
| **Failed** | The payment was not completed or was declined. You may need to retry with a different payment method. |

## Organizer Communications

Event organizers can post updates and important information — such as kit pickup times, route changes, or weather notices — that will appear alongside your registration in Meus Ingressos. Check back in the days before your event for any last-minute communications from the organizer.

## Common Status Questions

<AccordionGroup>
  <Accordion title="My payment status says 'pending' — am I registered?">
    A pending status after a boleto payment is completely normal. Boleto bancário payments can take **up to 3 business days** to be processed and confirmed by the banking system. Your registration is reserved while the payment clears. Once the bank confirms the payment, your status will automatically update to *confirmed* and you'll receive your confirmation email.

    If your status shows pending for Pix or credit card, this is unusual. Refresh the page and check again. If it remains pending after a few minutes, contact Atletis support at [contato@atletis.com.br](mailto:contato@atletis.com.br).
  </Accordion>

  <Accordion title="I paid my boleto more than 3 business days ago but the status is still 'pending' — what should I do?">
    If more than 3 business days have passed since you paid the boleto and your status in Meus Ingressos still shows *pending*, contact the Atletis support team at [contato@atletis.com.br](mailto:contato@atletis.com.br). Include your full name, the event name, the date you made the boleto payment, and any payment receipt you have. The team will investigate and update your registration accordingly.
  </Accordion>

  <Accordion title="My status is 'confirmed' but I didn't receive a confirmation email — is my registration valid?">
    Yes. If your status in Meus Ingressos shows *confirmed*, your registration is valid regardless of whether you received the email. The confirmation email may have been filtered into your spam or junk mail folder — check there and mark it as safe if you find it. Your QR code and registration details are always accessible directly from Meus Ingressos.
  </Accordion>

  <Accordion title="My payment status shows 'failed' — what should I do?">
    A *failed* status means your payment did not go through. Return to the event page and start the registration process again, choosing a different payment method if needed. Check that your card details are correct, or opt for Pix for an instant and reliable alternative. If you continue to experience issues, contact [contato@atletis.com.br](mailto:contato@atletis.com.br) for assistance.
  </Accordion>

  <Accordion title="I can't find a registration I completed — where is it?">
    Make sure you're logged in with the same email address you used when you registered. If you used a different email address at checkout, log in (or create an account) with that address and your registration will appear. If you're still unable to locate it, contact [contato@atletis.com.br](mailto:contato@atletis.com.br) with your full name, CPF, and the event name.
  </Accordion>
</AccordionGroup>
