When you register for a sports event on Atletis, you can choose from three payment methods: Pix, credit card, or boleto bancário (bank slip). Each method is processed through Atletis's secure, regulated payment infrastructure — the same protection applies regardless of which option you choose. This page explains how each method works, what to expect after payment, and what to do if something goes wrong.

## Payment Method Comparison

| | **Pix** | **Credit Card** | **Boleto Bancário** |
|---|---|---|---|
| **How to pay** | Scan QR code or copy the Pix key in your banking app | Enter card details at checkout | Generate the slip and pay at a bank, ATM, or banking app |
| **Confirmation speed** | Instant (seconds) | Instant (upon authorization) | Up to 3 business days |
| **Installments** | Not available | Available for higher-value registrations | Not available |
| **Deadline** | Up to the event's registration close | Up to the event's registration close | Only available up to **72 hours before the event** |
| **Best for** | Fast, hassle-free payment | Spreading cost over time | Those who prefer traditional bank slips |

## How Each Method Works

### Pix

Pix is Brazil's instant payment system and the fastest way to confirm your registration on Atletis.

1. At checkout, select **Pix** as your payment method.
2. A QR code and a copy-and-paste Pix key are displayed on screen.
3. Open your bank's app, navigate to the Pix section, and either scan the QR code or paste the key.
4. Confirm the payment in your banking app.
5. Your registration is confirmed within seconds, and the confirmation email is sent immediately.

### Credit Card

Credit card payment lets you complete checkout entirely within the Atletis platform without leaving the page.

1. At checkout, select **credit card** as your payment method.
2. Enter your card number, cardholder name, expiry date, and CVV.
3. For eligible higher-value registrations, you may see an option to split the amount into **installments**. Select the number of installments that works for you.
4. Confirm the payment. Your registration is confirmed upon card authorization, and the confirmation email is sent immediately.

### Boleto Bancário

Boleto is a traditional Brazilian payment method available at banks, ATMs, lottery houses, and most banking apps.

1. At checkout, select **boleto bancário** as your payment method.
2. A boleto is generated with a barcode and payment instructions.
3. Pay the boleto at your bank branch, ATM, internet banking portal, or banking app before the due date.
4. After payment, allow **up to 3 business days** for the bank to process and confirm it.
5. Once confirmed, your registration status in **Meus Ingressos** updates to *confirmed* and the confirmation email is sent.

<Warning>
  Boleto is only available for registrations made at least **72 hours before the event starts**. This cutoff exists because boleto payments can take up to 3 business days to clear — if you try to register within 72 hours of the event, the boleto option will not appear. In that case, use Pix or credit card for instant confirmation.
</Warning>

## After Payment: What Happens Next

Once your payment is confirmed (immediately for Pix and credit card; up to 3 business days for boleto):

- A **confirmation email** is sent to the address you provided during registration. It contains your registration details and a QR code.
- Your registration appears as **confirmed** in **Meus Ingressos** on [atletis.com.br](https://www.atletis.com.br).

## Service and Convenience Fee

Some event organizers choose to pass the platform service fee on to participants. When this applies to an event you're registering for, the fee is shown transparently in your order summary **before you confirm payment** — you'll never encounter a surprise charge after checkout. If you see a service fee and have questions about it, contact the event organizer or reach out to Atletis support at [contato@atletis.com.br](mailto:contato@atletis.com.br).

## Troubleshooting Payment Issues

If you're having trouble completing your payment:

1. **Double-check your payment details** — for credit card payments, verify the card number, expiry date, and CVV are entered correctly.
2. **Check if spots are still available** — events have limited registrations. If many people are buying at the same time, the last spots may sell out while you're in checkout. Refresh the page and start again to see the current availability.
3. **Try again** — refresh the page and restart the checkout process. Temporary errors sometimes resolve on their own.
4. **Contact support** — if the problem persists, email [contato@atletis.com.br](mailto:contato@atletis.com.br) and describe the issue, including the event name and the error message you received.
