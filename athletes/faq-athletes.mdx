Below you'll find answers to the most common questions athletes ask about using Atletis — from registering for an event and making a payment to finding your confirmation and managing your account. If your question isn't covered here, you can always reach the Atletis support team directly at [contato@atletis.com.br](mailto:contato@atletis.com.br).

<AccordionGroup>
  <Accordion title="How do I print my registration confirmation?">
    Your registration confirmation is available as soon as your payment is confirmed. You can access it in two ways:

    1. **From Meus Ingressos** — log in to [atletis.com.br](https://www.atletis.com.br), open **Meus Ingressos** (My Tickets), select the relevant event, and use the print or download option on your registration details page.
    2. **From your confirmation email** — Atletis sends a confirmation email to your registered email address as soon as payment is processed. The email contains your registration details and can be printed or forwarded directly.

    If your payment is still pending (for example, if you paid by boleto and the bank hasn't yet confirmed it), the confirmation and QR code will become available once the payment clears.
  </Accordion>

  <Accordion title="What payment methods are accepted?">
    Atletis accepts three payment methods for event registrations:

    - **Pix** — instant payment; your registration is confirmed within seconds.
    - **Credit card** — pay at checkout; installment options may be available for higher-value registrations.
    - **Boleto bancário** — generate a bank slip and pay at your bank, ATM, or banking app. Note that boleto is only available for registrations made at least **72 hours before the event starts**, and confirmation can take up to 3 business days.

    For a full comparison, see the [Payment Options](/athletes/payment-options) page.
  </Accordion>

  <Accordion title="How long until I receive my registration confirmation?">
    Your confirmation email is sent as soon as your payment is confirmed.

    - **Pix and credit card** — confirmation is immediate. Expect the email within a few minutes of completing checkout.
    - **Boleto bancário** — confirmation is sent after the bank processes the slip, which can take up to 3 business days after you make the payment.

    In all cases, you can check your current registration status in **Meus Ingressos** at any time without waiting for the email.
  </Accordion>

  <Accordion title="Is it safe to buy on Atletis?">
    Yes, it's completely safe. Atletis uses SSL encryption on all pages and processes payments through regulated payment infrastructure designed for this type of transaction. Your card details are never stored on the platform, and your personal data is handled in accordance with Atletis's Privacy Policy. Thousands of athletes across Brazil have already used the platform to register for events.
  </Accordion>

  <Accordion title="I registered but didn't receive a confirmation email — what happened?">
    If you haven't received a confirmation email within a few minutes of completing your registration:

    1. **Check your spam or junk mail folder** — confirmation emails can be filtered depending on your email provider's settings. If you find the email there, mark it as "not spam" so future emails arrive in your inbox.
    2. **Check Meus Ingressos** — log in to [atletis.com.br](https://www.atletis.com.br) and go to **Meus Ingressos** (My Tickets). Your registration and its payment status are always visible here. If the status shows *confirmed*, your registration is valid even without the email.

    If your registration doesn't appear in Meus Ingressos either, contact [contato@atletis.com.br](mailto:contato@atletis.com.br) with your name, CPF, and the event you registered for.
  </Accordion>

  <Accordion title="I paid my boleto but the status is still 'pending' — why?">
    This is normal. Boleto bancário payments are processed by the banking system and typically take **up to 3 business days** to be confirmed after you make the payment. During this time, your registration status will show as *pending* in Meus Ingressos.

    Once the bank confirms the payment, your status will automatically update to *confirmed* and your confirmation email will be sent.

    If more than 3 business days have passed since you paid and the status still shows *pending*, please contact the Atletis support team at [contato@atletis.com.br](mailto:contato@atletis.com.br) with your payment details so they can investigate.
  </Accordion>

  <Accordion title="The event I want to participate in is free — do I still need to register?">
    Yes. Even for free events, you must complete the registration process through the Atletis platform. Registration is important for two reasons:

    1. **Spots may be limited** — the number of free registrations can be capped, and only completing registration guarantees your place.
    2. **Organizer planning** — the organizer relies on the registration count to plan logistics, supplies, and staffing for the event. Without your registration, they can't accurately know how many people will attend.

    The registration process for a free event is the same as for a paid event, except no payment step is required.
  </Accordion>

  <Accordion title="I'm having trouble completing my payment — what should I do?">
    If you're experiencing issues at checkout, try the following steps:

    1. **Check your payment details** — for credit card payments, verify that the card number, cardholder name, expiry date, and CVV are entered correctly.
    2. **Check spot availability** — if multiple people are registering at the same time, the last available spots may sell out while you're in checkout. Refresh the page to restart the process and check current availability.
    3. **Try a different payment method** — if your card is being declined, switching to Pix is a reliable alternative with instant confirmation.
    4. **Contact support** — if the issue persists, email [contato@atletis.com.br](mailto:contato@atletis.com.br) with the event name and a description of the error you're seeing.
  </Accordion>

  <Accordion title="Can I transfer my registration to someone else?">
    Registration transfer policies are set by individual event organizers. To find out whether a transfer is possible for your specific event, contact the event organizer directly (their contact information is usually on the event page) or reach out to Atletis support at [contato@atletis.com.br](mailto:contato@atletis.com.br), who can help facilitate the conversation with the organizer.
  </Accordion>

  <Accordion title="How do I contact Atletis support?">
    You can reach the Atletis support team by email at [contato@atletis.com.br](mailto:contato@atletis.com.br). Include as much detail as possible about your question or issue — your full name, the event name, and any relevant registration or payment information — so the team can assist you quickly.
  </Accordion>
</AccordionGroup>
