Atletis brings together sports events from all over Brazil on a single platform. Whether you're looking for a 5K running race in your city, a cycling stage, a volleyball tournament, or an aquatic festival, the event discovery page at [atletis.com.br/eventos](https://www.atletis.com.br/eventos) gives you a live, up-to-date calendar of everything currently open for registration. Each listing is published directly by the event organizer, so all the information you see — dates, location, photos, and registration links — reflects exactly what the organizer has set up.

## Browsing the Events Page

Head to [atletis.com.br/eventos](https://www.atletis.com.br/eventos) to see all available events. The page displays a paginated list of upcoming events, currently featuring more than 1,200 events across Brazil, with new ones added regularly.

Each event card on the listing shows:

| Field | What you'll see |
|---|---|
| **Event photo** | A banner image uploaded by the organizer |
| **Event name** | The official name of the race or competition |
| **Date** | The day and start time of the event |
| **Location** | City and state (e.g., *Mogi das Cruzes/São Paulo*) |
| **"Confira este evento" button** | A direct link to the full event page |

## Discovering Your Next Event

<Steps>
  <Step title="Go to the Events Page">
    Navigate to [atletis.com.br/eventos](https://www.atletis.com.br/eventos) in your browser. You'll see all upcoming events sorted by date, with the nearest dates appearing first.
  </Step>

  <Step title="Scan the Listings">
    Scroll through the event cards. Each card shows the event photo, name, date, start time, and city/state at a glance. The date badge in the top-left corner of each photo makes it easy to spot events coming up in a particular month.
  </Step>

  <Step title="Open an Event Page">
    Click **"Confira este evento"** (or anywhere on the event card) to open the full event page. Here you'll find everything the organizer has published, including:

    - **Categories** — age groups, genders, and distances available
    - **Registration pricing** — current batch price and upcoming price changes
    - **Event schedule** — start times, check-in windows, and award ceremonies
    - **Route and regulations** — course maps and participation rules (where provided by the organizer)
    - **Registration button** — to begin your sign-up
  </Step>

  <Step title="Check All the Details">
    Review the full event page carefully before registering. Pay attention to the available categories, the current registration price (batch pricing means the price may increase as registration fills up), and any specific requirements listed by the organizer — such as minimum age, medical clearance, or team formation rules.
  </Step>

  <Step title="Start Your Registration">
    When you're ready, click the registration button on the event page to begin the sign-up process. See the [Registering for an Event](/athletes/registration-process) guide for a full walkthrough of the checkout flow.
  </Step>
</Steps>

## Sharing Events with Training Partners

Every event on Atletis has its own unique URL (for example, `atletis.com.br/evento/etapa-pedra-grande-2026-662`). Copy the link from your browser's address bar and share it directly via WhatsApp, Telegram, or any other app to invite your training partners to sign up alongside you.

<Tip>
  Sign up for the Atletis email list to receive exclusive content and be among the first to hear about new events. Look for the email subscription form at the bottom of any page on [atletis.com.br](https://www.atletis.com.br) and enter your name and email to subscribe for free.
</Tip>
