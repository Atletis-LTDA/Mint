Registering for a sports event on Atletis is designed to be quick and straightforward. The entire process — from choosing your category to receiving your confirmation email — happens on the platform, so you never need to deal with paper forms, bank transfers to strangers, or unconfirmed messages. This guide walks you through every step so you know exactly what to expect before you begin.

<Note>
  Each CPF (Brazilian individual taxpayer ID) can only be used to register once per event. This rule prevents duplicate registrations and ensures the organizer has an accurate list of participants. If your CPF has already been used for a given event, the platform will notify you at the point of registration.
</Note>

## Full Registration Flow

<Steps>
  <Step title="Find the Event and Open Its Page">
    Go to [atletis.com.br/eventos](https://www.atletis.com.br/eventos) and browse or search for the event you want to join. Click **"Confira este evento"** to open the event's dedicated page, where you'll find full details: categories, pricing, schedule, and any rules set by the organizer. When you're ready, click the **registration button** on the event page.
  </Step>

  <Step title="Choose Your Category">
    Most events offer multiple categories based on factors such as age group, gender, and distance. Select the category that matches your profile. Read each category description carefully — choosing the wrong category may affect your eligibility for awards and could prevent the organizer from confirming your spot.
  </Step>

  <Step title="Fill in Your Registration Form">
    Complete the registration form with your personal details. Fields typically include:

    - **Full name**
    - **CPF** (Brazilian taxpayer ID)
    - **Date of birth**
    - **Phone number**
    - **Email address**
    - **Any custom fields** added by the organizer (such as team name, t-shirt size, or emergency contact)

    Fill in all required fields accurately. Your CPF is used to identify your registration uniquely within the event, and your email is where the confirmation will be sent.
  </Step>

  <Step title="Complete the Medical Form (If Required)">
    Some events require participants to complete a medical or health declaration form as part of the registration process. If the organizer has enabled this, you'll see the medical form as an additional step before checkout. Fill it in honestly — this information is used by the organizer for participant safety and event planning.
  </Step>

  <Step title="Select Your Payment Method">
    Choose how you'd like to pay from the available options:

    - **Pix** — instant payment; confirmation is immediate
    - **Credit card** — enter your card details at checkout; installment options may be available for higher-value registrations
    - **Boleto bancário** — generate a bank slip and pay at a bank, ATM, or banking app; note that boleto is only available up to 72 hours before the event start time, and confirmation can take up to 3 business days

    For full details on each payment method, see the [Payment Options](/athletes/payment-options) guide.
  </Step>

  <Step title="Review and Complete Checkout">
    Before confirming your purchase, review your order summary. This screen shows your chosen event, category, and the total amount due — including any service or convenience fee that the organizer has chosen to pass on to participants. This fee is displayed transparently before you confirm, so there are no surprises. When everything looks correct, complete the checkout.
  </Step>

  <Step title="Receive Your Confirmation Email">
    As soon as your payment is confirmed, Atletis sends a confirmation email to the address you provided during registration. The email contains:

    - Your registration details (event name, date, location, category)
    - A **QR code** for use on event day (where applicable)
    - A link to **Meus Ingressos** (My Tickets) where you can view and manage your registration

    Your registration will also appear immediately in **Meus Ingressos** on [atletis.com.br](https://www.atletis.com.br) when you log in.
  </Step>
</Steps>

## Registering for Free Events

If the event you want to join is free of charge, you still need to complete the registration process through the platform. Registration — even for free events — is required because the organizer needs an accurate headcount to plan and run the event safely. The process is identical to a paid event, except no payment step is required.

## Didn't Receive Your Confirmation Email?

If your confirmation email hasn't arrived within a few minutes of completing checkout:

1. **Check your spam or junk mail folder** — confirmation emails occasionally land there depending on your email provider's filters.
2. **Log in to Meus Ingressos** at [atletis.com.br](https://www.atletis.com.br) — your registration and its current status are always available there, regardless of whether the email was delivered.

If your registration shows as confirmed in Meus Ingressos, you're all set. If the status is still pending after following the relevant payment steps, see the [My Tickets](/athletes/my-tickets) guide for next steps.
