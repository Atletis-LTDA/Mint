Creating and publishing a sports event on Atletis is designed to be fast. You don't need technical knowledge or an existing website — Atletis gives you a dedicated event page, a registration flow, and a payment system out of the box. Follow the steps below and you'll have your event live and accepting registrations in under 10 minutes.

<Note>
  Before you start, gather the key details about your event: name, date, location, sport type, category definitions (e.g., age groups, distances), planned pricing, and your logo or banner image. Having this information ready will make the setup process even faster.
</Note>

## Step-by-Step Setup

<Steps>
  <Step title="Create Your Free Account">
    Go to [app.atletis.com.br/evento/criar](http://app.atletis.com.br/evento/criar) and register for a free account. Fill in your details to create a single login that lets you manage multiple events from the same dashboard.

    Once you log in, you'll see your organizer dashboard — the central hub for all your events, registrations, and reports.

    <Tip>
      Your account is free and gives you access to all of Atletis's core features. You are only charged when a paid registration is successfully completed.
    </Tip>
  </Step>

  <Step title="Fill In Your Event Information">
    Start the event creation flow and complete all required fields:

    - **Event name** — the public title participants will see
    - **Date and time** — when the competition takes place
    - **Location** — city, state, and venue
    - **Sport type** — select from the available sports (running, cycling, triathlon, volleyball, football, and more)
    - **Description** — rules, course details, kit information, or anything else athletes need to know
    - **Banner image / logo** — give your event page a professional look

    <Tip>
      You can also choose between a **public event** (visible on the Atletis events calendar and shareable via direct link) and a **private event** (only accessible to people you send the link to). Private events are useful for club competitions or invitation-only races.
    </Tip>
  </Step>

  <Step title="Create Your Categories">
    Add the registration categories that apply to your event. Common segmentation options include:

    - Age group (e.g., 18–29, 30–39, 40+)
    - Gender (male, female, mixed)
    - Distance or modality (e.g., 5K, 10K, 21K)
    - Skill or experience level

    For each category, you can add a description and set a maximum participant limit. Category limits help you manage logistics and avoid overselling specific groups.

    <Tip>
      Setting per-category limits is especially useful for events with physical constraints — such as limited kit sizes, lane capacity in a pool, or field sizes in team sports.
    </Tip>
  </Step>

  <Step title="Set Pricing and Batch (Lote) Transitions">
    Configure the registration price for each category. Atletis supports **multiple pricing batches (lotes)** within a single event, which lets you offer early-bird pricing and automatically increase prices as the event approaches or as spots fill up.

    For each batch, define:

    - **Price** — the registration amount in Brazilian Reais (R$)
    - **Transition trigger** — choose between a specific **date/time** or a **maximum number of registrations** sold

    When the trigger condition is met, Atletis automatically activates the next batch and updates the price on your event page — no manual action needed.

    <Tip>
      A common strategy is to open with a low early-bird price to build momentum, then schedule two or three batch transitions leading up to the event. This creates urgency and rewards participants who register early.
    </Tip>
  </Step>

  <Step title="Choose Payment Methods">
    Select which payment methods you want to offer to participants:

    - **Pix** — instant payment, confirmed in seconds
    - **Credit card** — including installment payments; you receive the full amount upfront
    - **Boleto bancário** — bank slip payment (note: boleto can only be used up to 72 hours before the event, due to processing time)

    You also decide whether to **pass the Atletis service fee on to the participant** (as a convenience fee) or to **absorb it yourself**. Both options are available and configurable per event.

    <Note>
      If your event registrations are free, there is no charge at all — not for you and not for the participant.
    </Note>
  </Step>

  <Step title="Review Your Event">
    Before publishing, review all the details you've entered:

    - Event information (name, date, location, sport)
    - Categories and their descriptions or limits
    - Pricing batches and transition rules
    - Enabled payment methods

    Make any final adjustments at this stage. Everything can be edited before you go live.
  </Step>

  <Step title="Publish and Share">
    Click **"Publicar evento"** (Publish event). Your event is immediately:

    - Listed on the [Atletis events calendar](https://www.atletis.com.br/eventos) (if public)
    - Accessible via a direct link you can share on social media, WhatsApp, or email

    You can copy and share your event URL right away. Participants can register and pay directly from your event page.
  </Step>
</Steps>

## What Happens After You Publish

Once your event is live, Atletis handles the operational side automatically:

- **Confirmation emails** — every participant receives an automated confirmation email with event details and their registration PDF as soon as payment is approved.
- **Real-time dashboard** — your organizer panel shows registration counts, payment statuses, and revenue figures updated in real time.
- **Automatic batch transitions** — if you configured lote transitions, prices update automatically without any action from you.
- **Participant communication** — you can send messages or updates to registered participants directly from your dashboard.
- **Check-in on race day** — use the Atletis check-in app to validate participants at the venue quickly and accurately.

<Tip>
  After the event, export your full participant list and financial report from the dashboard. These reports include registration data per category, payment method breakdowns, and totals — useful for post-event analysis and planning your next edition.
</Tip>
