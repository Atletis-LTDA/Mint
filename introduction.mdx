Atletis is a Brazilian sports event management platform built for two groups: event organizers who need a reliable, end-to-end tool to create, promote, and run competitions — and athletes who want a single place to discover and register for their next challenge. Whether you're launching a neighborhood 5K or a multi-category triathlon with hundreds of participants, Atletis handles registration, payments, check-in, and reporting so you can focus on delivering a great event.

## How the Organizer Workflow Works

Here is a bird's-eye view of the journey from idea to live event on Atletis:

1. **Create a free account** at [app.atletis.com.br/evento/criar](http://app.atletis.com.br/evento/criar) — no credit card required.
2. **Fill in your event details** — name, date, location, sport type, description, and banner image.
3. **Set up categories** — define age groups, distances, genders, or any other segmentation your competition requires.
4. **Configure pricing and batches (lotes)** — set early-bird prices and schedule automatic transitions to higher-priced batches.
5. **Choose payment methods** — enable Pix, credit card (including installments), and boleto bancário.
6. **Review and publish** — your event goes live on the Atletis events calendar and you can share the direct link immediately.

<Note>
  Registrations for free events cost you nothing. For paid events, Atletis charges a small per-registration fee only when a registration is successfully sold — no monthly subscription, no setup fee. See the [Pricing](/pricing) page for full details.
</Note>
