Every registration that comes through Atletis immediately becomes part of your event's participant list — a live record that grows as sales come in and updates the moment a payment is confirmed. You can access this list from your organizer dashboard at any time, filter it by any field, export it for offline use, and share a public version with participants who want to see who else has signed up. This page explains what the list contains, how to work with it, and how to get it into the hands of your team before race day.

## What the participant list includes

The list reflects the data collected during registration. Depending on the fields configured in your registration form, each participant record can include:

- **Full name** and **CPF** (Brazilian tax ID) — used for identity verification at check-in
- **Category** and **batch (*lote*)** — used for wave assignments and pricing records
- **Payment status** — paid, pending, or failed
- **Registration date** — useful for resolving disputes about batch pricing
- **Custom form answers** — any additional fields you added to your form, such as kit size (T-shirt size), team name, emergency contact, or medical information

The list is the single source of truth for your event. Because it is generated directly from registrations, it eliminates the need to reconcile a spreadsheet against a payments report — everything is in one place.

## Accessing and filtering the list

Open your **organizer dashboard**, navigate to your event, and select the **Participants** (Inscritos) section. The full list appears with all registered participants.

Use the filter controls to narrow the list by:

- **Category** — show only participants in a specific competitive group
- **Payment status** — isolate paid participants for check-in prep, or surface pending payments that need follow-up
- **Batch (*lote*)** — review who registered in each pricing period
- **Custom fields** — filter by kit size, team, or any other field you collected

Filters can be combined. For example, you can view all paid participants in the Women's 5K category who selected a size M kit — exactly the slice of data you need when separating kits by category and size.

## Exporting the participant list

<Steps>
  <Step title="Open the Participants section">
    In your organizer dashboard, navigate to your event and click **Participants**. The full list loads with all current registrations.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Apply any filters you need">
    If you want to export only a specific category or payment status, apply those filters before exporting. The exported file will contain only the rows currently visible in the filtered view.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Click Export">
    Select the **Export** option to download the list. The file includes all visible columns — name, CPF, category, batch, payment status, registration date, and any custom form answers.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Use the file for logistics">
    Import the exported file into your team's preferred tool for kit separation, bib number assignment, or volunteer briefing packets. For check-in coordination, share the exported list with team leads so they know exactly who to expect and in which category.
  </Step>
</Steps>

<Tip>
  **Kit organization tip:** export the list filtered by category and then sort by kit size before printing the kit separation sheet. Group all kits for each category together and then stack by size within each group. This way your kit table mirrors the order the list is sorted — volunteers can pull from the pile without searching, and the flow stays fast even during peak arrival times.
</Tip>

## Real-time updates

The participant list updates automatically every time a new registration is completed and payment is confirmed. You do not need to refresh manually — the dashboard reflects the current state of registrations at any moment you open it.

This means you can check the list the morning of the event and have a fully current picture of who is registered. If a participant paid by boleto (*bank slip*) and payment was confirmed overnight, they will appear as paid in the list by the time your team arrives at the venue.

<Note>
  Boleto (*bank slip*) payments can take up to three business days to clear. Participants who paid by boleto close to the event date may show a **pending** status on event day. Check the list in the dashboard for the latest status before making entry decisions at the gate.
</Note>

## Public participant list

When enabled, the public participant list lets registered participants see who else has signed up for your event. You can turn this feature on or off from your event settings.

Organizers of running races and cycling events often enable this feature as a social proof tool — athletes like to see familiar names on the list and it can encourage late registrations from people in the same running club or cycling group.

## Using the list for check-in coordination

The participant list and the check-in app draw from the same data source. Any filters or notes you apply in the dashboard are immediately available to the check-in app on volunteers' devices.

Before the event, share the exported list with your check-in team leads so they can:

- Understand the expected volume and category mix
- Identify any participants with pending payment that may need special handling at the gate
- Plan how many check-in stations and volunteers are needed per category or alphabetical group
- Brief exception handlers on which participants to expect at the resolution desk

A team that has reviewed the list before the gates open makes faster, more consistent decisions on race morning.
