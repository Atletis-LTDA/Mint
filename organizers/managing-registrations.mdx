Once your event is live and registrations start coming in, the Atletis organizer dashboard becomes your command center. Every registration, payment confirmation, and category assignment flows into a single panel at [app.atletis.com.br/meus-eventos](https://app.atletis.com.br/meus-eventos), so you can always see exactly where you stand — without spreadsheets, manual confirmation messages, or guesswork. This page walks you through the key workflows you'll use from the moment registrations open to the day you hand out race kits.

## The organizer dashboard overview

After logging in, navigate to **Meus Eventos** and select your event. The event management panel gives you access to:

- **Resumo (Summary)** — a snapshot of total registrations, revenue, and sales by lote and category
- **Inscrições (Registrations)** — the full list of registered participants with individual details
- **Cupons** — coupon creation and usage tracking
- **Financeiro (Financial)** — revenue reports and payout information

<Tip>
  Bookmark your event's direct dashboard URL so you can check sales in real time from your phone between meetings, at the venue during setup, or anywhere else. Atletis is accessible on mobile browsers without needing a separate app for the organizer panel.
</Tip>

## Tracking sales in real time

The **Resumo** section updates continuously as new registrations come in. From here you can see:

- Total number of confirmed registrations
- Total revenue collected so far
- Breakdown of registrations by **category** (e.g. 5 km, 10 km, kids' race)
- Breakdown of registrations by **lote** (e.g. how many came from Lote 1 vs. Lote 2)

Watching the pace at which each lote fills lets you make informed decisions: if Lote 1 is selling faster than expected, you might tighten the slot limit; if it's moving slowly, you may need to ramp up promotion before the transition date.

## Viewing individual registrations

<Steps>
  <Step title="Open the Inscrições tab">
    Inside your event panel, click **Inscrições** in the sidebar. You'll see a paginated list of all registered participants.
  </Step>

  <Step title="Review registration details">
    Each row in the list shows:
    - Participant name
    - CPF (Brazilian tax ID)
    - Chosen category and lote
    - Payment method and payment status (confirmed, pending, or failed)
    - Registration date and time
  </Step>

  <Step title="Search and filter">
    Use the search bar to find a participant by name or CPF. You can also filter the list by:
    - Payment status (confirmed / pending)
    - Category
    - Lote

    Filtering by "pending" is particularly useful in the days leading up to the event to identify registrations that may need follow-up (for example, unpaid boletos).
  </Step>
</Steps>

## CPF-unique control

Atletis enforces **one registration per CPF per event** by default. If a participant tries to register a second time using the same CPF, the platform blocks the duplicate. This protects you from:

- Accidental double purchases (and the refund headaches that follow)
- Fraudulent use of cortesia or discount coupons across multiple registrations
- Inflated participant counts that distort your logistics planning

<Note>
  CPF-unique control applies per event. The same participant can register for different events — or for the same recurring event in a future edition — without issue.
</Note>

## Handling exceptional cases

### Pending boleto registrations

A registration paid by boleto will appear as **pending** in your list until the bank confirms the payment (up to 3 business days). During this window:

- The slot is considered reserved, even though payment isn't confirmed yet.
- If you're tracking available spots, count pending boletos as tentative registrations.
- If the boleto is not paid within the payment deadline, the status automatically updates to cancelled and the slot is released.

<Warning>
  Do not manually cancel a pending boleto registration unless you have confirmed with the participant that they do not intend to pay. Cancelling a registration that the participant has already paid at a bank (but whose payment hasn't cleared yet) will cause a support issue.
</Warning>

### Registrations that need a category correction

If a participant registered in the wrong category (for example, the wrong age group or wrong distance), contact them directly and ask them to reach out to Atletis support at [contato@atletis.com.br](mailto:contato@atletis.com.br) to discuss the correction. Keep a note of the request in your own records.

## Sending messages to registered participants

Atletis centralizes communication with your participants so you don't have to manage a WhatsApp group or BCC email list manually. From the event panel, you can send updates directly to all confirmed registrants — useful for:

- Announcing venue changes or schedule adjustments
- Sharing kit pickup information and logistics details
- Sending countdown reminders as the event date approaches
- Notifying participants of lote transitions

<Tip>
  Send at least one logistical update in the week before the event (location, start time, parking, kit pickup). Participants who feel well-informed arrive prepared, and your team spends less time answering the same questions on the day.
</Tip>

## Exporting the participant list

When it's time to prepare race kits, bib numbers, or check-in sheets, you can export a complete participant list from the dashboard.

<Steps>
  <Step title="Go to Inscrições">
    Open the **Inscrições** tab in your event panel.
  </Step>

  <Step title="Apply any filters you need">
    Filter by category, lote, or payment status to export only the data you need. For kit preparation, export confirmed registrations only.
  </Step>

  <Step title="Export the list">
    Click the export button to download the participant data. The export includes name, CPF, category, lote, shirt size (if collected), and payment status, giving your logistics team everything they need to prepare kits accurately.
  </Step>
</Steps>

<Tip>
  Export the list at least 48 hours before the event and again on the morning of the event to capture any last-minute registrations. Keep both versions on hand — the earlier export is useful for kit preparation, while the final export is the authoritative list for check-in.
</Tip>
