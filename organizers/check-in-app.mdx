Race day check-in only runs smoothly when the groundwork was laid before the start gun. The Atletis check-in app connects directly to the centralized participant list created by your registrations, so every volunteer working a credentialing station is looking at the same up-to-date data — no printed spreadsheets, no conflicting versions, no last-minute reconciliation. This page explains how the app works, how to get your team set up, and what to do when edge cases appear at the entrance gate.

## What the check-in app does

The app identifies each participant against your registered participant list, marks them as arrived the moment they are confirmed, and makes that status visible to every device logged into your event in real time. Because the list comes directly from Atletis registrations, the same data your participants used to sign up — name, CPF (Brazilian tax ID), category, batch (*lote*), and payment status — is what your volunteers see when someone walks up to the gate.

Participants receive a confirmation email when their payment is approved. That email contains a QR code linked to their registration. The app reads that QR code and checks the participant in instantly. When a QR code is not available, volunteers can search manually by name or CPF.

## Accessing the app

The check-in app is included with every event created on Atletis at no additional charge. To get your team ready:

- Each volunteer downloads the Atletis check-in app on their smartphone or tablet.
- You share your event credentials with the volunteers you want to grant access.
- Multiple team members can run the app on separate devices at the same time — all check-ins are reflected in the same list in real time.

There is no limit to the number of devices that can be active simultaneously, which means you can open as many entry points as your venue requires.

## Setting up check-in on event day

<Steps>
  <Step title="Log in to the app">
    Each volunteer opens the Atletis check-in app and logs in with the credentials you have shared. Make sure every device is connected to the internet before participants begin arriving.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Select your event">
    From the app home screen, tap the event you are running. The full participant list loads automatically from the Atletis platform — this is the same list visible in your organizer dashboard.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Assign roles at each station">
    Before the gates open, confirm which volunteers are checking IDs, which are scanning QR codes, and who is handling exceptions. Having a dedicated person for exceptions prevents a single edge case from creating a queue at the main gate.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Scan or search for each participant">
    Point the app camera at the QR code in the participant's confirmation email. The app confirms the registration and marks the participant as arrived. If the QR code is unavailable — phone battery dead, email not found — use the search field to look up the participant by name or CPF.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Separate kit pickup from credentialing">
    Once a participant is checked in, direct them to the kit pickup area as a distinct physical step. Mixing ID verification and kit delivery at the same station creates a double bottleneck. Keep the flows separate so neither line stalls the other.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Monitor attendance in real time">
    At any point during the event, you can open your organizer dashboard to see how many participants have checked in, broken down by category. This information is useful for pacing the start waves and planning logistics during the event.
  </Step>
</Steps>

## Handling edge cases at the gate

Not every participant will arrive with a QR code ready and a confirmed payment. Agree on a response for each scenario before the gates open — a rule decided in advance keeps the line moving and prevents different volunteers from making contradictory calls.

| Situation | Likely cause | Recommended action |
|---|---|---|
| Participant not found in the list | Registration may be unpaid or incomplete | Search by CPF to verify; if absent, direct to the exceptions desk before allowing entry |
| Participant found but payment pending | Payment not yet confirmed (e.g., boleto not yet cleared) | Follow your event's policy — check the dashboard for the latest payment status before deciding |
| Participant already marked as checked in | Possible duplicate attempt or a team member already scanned them | Confirm with the participant and check the timestamp in the app; do not check in twice |
| QR code won't scan | Poor lighting, damaged screen, or old screenshot | Switch to manual search by name or CPF |
| Name mismatch | Participant registered under another person's name | Verify CPF and cross-reference the registration details in the app |

<Note>
  **Offline contingency:** if the venue's internet connection drops, download or print a backup copy of the full participant list from your organizer dashboard the night before the event. Assign one volunteer to manage the offline list manually and reconcile the paper records with the digital list as soon as connectivity is restored. Even when offline, any check-ins logged in the app will sync automatically once the connection returns.
</Note>

## Pre-event planning for a smoother gate

The app handles identification quickly, but the speed of your gate depends as much on physical setup and team preparation as on the technology.

**Define team roles before the day.** Decide how many volunteers you need per check-in point based on expected attendance and arrival windows. A single volunteer handling 200 arrivals in a 30-minute peak will create a queue regardless of how fast the app responds. A general guideline: plan for one active scanning station per 50–75 participants expected to arrive during peak hour.

**Separate check-in from kit pickup.** Confirm that each flow has a clear physical path with visible signage. A participant who passes check-in and then joins a kit queue at the same counter slows down the people directly behind them.

**Brief your team on exception rules.** Walk every volunteer through the edge-case table above before the gates open. Ten minutes of alignment before the event prevents ad-hoc decisions that produce inconsistent results — and frustrated participants.

**Test the app before the day.** Log in to the app the evening before and verify that your participant list has loaded correctly. Check that QR code scanning works on each device under different lighting conditions.
