Every event created on Atletis gets its own dedicated public page with a unique URL. This page is the primary place athletes go to understand your event, evaluate whether it's right for them, and complete their registration. The more complete and clearly organized your event page is, the fewer questions land in your WhatsApp inbox — and the more registrations you convert.

Your event page is also listed in the Atletis event calendar at [atletis.com.br/eventos](https://www.atletis.com.br/eventos), giving your competition extra visibility to thousands of athletes already browsing the platform.

## Customizing your event page

You can update all sections of your event page at any time from the organizer dashboard. Here's what you can configure in each area:

### Event description and cover image

Your description introduces the event to athletes who may never have heard of it before. Write 2–4 focused paragraphs that explain what the event is, who it's for, what makes it worth entering, and any critical logistics athletes should know in advance.

Your cover image appears at the top of the page and as the thumbnail in the Atletis calendar listing. Use a high-quality, wide-format photo — an aerial shot of the course, an action photo from a prior edition, or professional event artwork.

<Tip>
  Avoid putting important text inside your cover image. It will be cropped differently on mobile screens and in calendar thumbnails. Keep text in the description field instead.
</Tip>

### Event schedule (programação)

The schedule section lists every activity that takes place before, during, and after your event in chronological order. A well-structured schedule reduces athlete questions dramatically.

For each activity, include:
- **Activity name** (e.g., "Kit pickup", "Check-in opens", "5 km wave start", "Awards ceremony")
- **Start time and end time**
- **Location**, if different from the main venue

<Tip>
  Publish your schedule as early as possible, even if some details are still being confirmed. Indicate "TBC" on uncertain times rather than leaving the schedule empty. Athletes plan travel, accommodation, and preparation around the schedule.
</Tip>

### Course map

Upload or link your course map directly on the event page. For running and cycling events, a map showing the route and elevation profile helps athletes prepare their training and logistics. For multi-sport events, annotate each segment of the course.

### Sponsors and partners

Add sponsor logos with optional links to their websites. Sponsors value the visibility that comes with having their brand shown on your event page, which is also distributed in the Atletis calendar to a sports-engaged audience.

### Photo gallery

Upload photos from previous editions of your event, or from the venue and course. A gallery with real race-day images gives new athletes a genuine sense of the experience — atmosphere, size, and vibe — that text alone cannot convey.

## Sharing your event link

Once your event is published, Atletis generates a unique URL for your event page in the format:

```
atletis.com.br/evento/your-event-name-XXXX
```

Share this link anywhere you promote your event: social media posts, WhatsApp groups, Instagram bio, email campaigns, partner websites, and printed materials. The link always points to the same page, so registrations, schedule updates, and photo additions are visible to anyone who visits.

<Note>
  You can copy your event's direct link from the **Summary** tab in your organizer dashboard at any time.
</Note>

## Public vs. private events

When you create your event, you choose whether it is **public** or **private**. This setting controls whether your event appears in the Atletis public calendar.

| | **Public** | **Private** |
|---|---|---|
| Listed on atletis.com.br/eventos | ✅ Yes | ❌ No |
| Accessible via direct link | ✅ Yes | ✅ Yes |
| Discoverable by athletes browsing Atletis | ✅ Yes | ❌ No |
| Best for | Open competitions, community races | Internal events, club competitions, invite-only races |

For **private events**, only people who receive the direct link can access the registration page. The event is not indexed or listed anywhere on the Atletis platform. This is ideal for corporate events, school competitions, or any event where you want to control who sees and registers.

You can change an event from private to public (or vice versa) at any time from your organizer dashboard.

## The Atletis event calendar

Public events are automatically listed on [atletis.com.br/eventos](https://www.atletis.com.br/eventos), a browsable directory of upcoming sports competitions across Brazil. Athletes use this calendar to discover new events by date, location, and sport modality. Being listed here at no additional cost gives your event organic reach beyond your existing audience.

Your event appears in the calendar with its cover image, name, date, city, and start time. Clicking the listing takes the athlete directly to your event page.

<Tip>
  Complete your event page — especially the cover image, description, and schedule — before publishing. Listings with compelling images and clear descriptions attract more clicks from athletes browsing the calendar.
</Tip>
