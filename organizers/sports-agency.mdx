In Brazilian sports culture, an *assessoria esportiva* (sports coaching agency) is far more than a personal trainer. It's a team of professionals — coaches, trainers, and sometimes nutritionists or physiotherapists — who manage groups of athletes through training programs, race preparation, and event participation. When race day comes, the assessoria often coordinates not just the training but the entire event journey: registration, kit collection, logistics, and on-site support.

Atletis is built with this reality in mind. The platform includes a simplified bulk purchase flow specifically designed for assessorias, so a coordinator can register an entire group of athletes in a single transaction — no chasing down individual payments, no asking each athlete to navigate checkout on their own.

## What Is an Assessoria Esportiva?

An assessoria esportiva is a sports coaching and training agency responsible for preparing groups of athletes — including recreational and amateur competitors — for their chosen sport and events. Assessorias may offer:

- Personalized or group training plans (in-person or remote)
- Guidance on which events to enter and when
- Help with registration and event logistics
- On-site support during competitions, including tents, equipment, and sometimes photography

When an assessoria partners with an event organizer, it acts as a powerful distribution channel: it brings motivated, well-prepared athletes directly to the event, while handling much of the registration coordination on their behalf.

## The Bulk Purchase Flow for Assessorias

Atletis makes group registration straightforward. Instead of each athlete completing a separate checkout, the assessoria coordinator purchases multiple registrations in a single cart transaction.

<Note>
  This differs from individual registration in one key way: the coordinator is the purchaser of record for the entire group. Each athlete's registration data (name, category, CPF, and any custom form fields) is still collected per participant, but payment flows through a single transaction — saving time and eliminating coordination overhead.
</Note>

<Steps>
  <Step title="Find the event on Atletis">
    Navigate to the event page on atletis.com.br or use the direct link shared by the event organizer. Confirm the event details, available categories, and current registration batch (lote) pricing.
  </Step>

  <Step title="Add registrations to your cart">
    On the registration page, add the number of registrations your group needs to the cart — one per athlete you are registering. Atletis supports simplified bulk purchases for assessorias so you can complete the entire group in a single transaction.
  </Step>

  <Step title="Fill in each athlete's registration details">
    For each registration in the cart, provide the required information: the athlete's name, CPF (Brazilian tax ID), category, and any additional fields the organizer requires (such as t-shirt size or emergency contact). Each athlete's data is recorded individually, even though you're completing a single checkout.
  </Step>

  <Step title="Complete a single checkout">
    Proceed through checkout as a single transaction covering all registrations in the cart. Atletis accepts credit card, Pix, and bank slip (boleto bancário) — note that boleto payments must be completed at least 72 hours before the event to allow for processing time.
  </Step>

  <Step title="Receive group confirmation">
    Once payment is confirmed, every athlete in the group receives a registration confirmation email with their individual event details. You, as the coordinator, have a complete record of all registrations made under this transaction — keep it on hand for kit collection, check-in coordination, and any follow-up with the event organizer, such as arranging group discount codes or reserved space on race day.
  </Step>
</Steps>

## Benefits for Agency Coordinators

**Saves time** — Instead of forwarding a registration link to a dozen athletes and waiting for each one to complete checkout individually, you handle the entire group in one sitting.

**Ensures correct registration** — You control the data entered for each athlete, reducing errors in category assignment, CPF fields, and custom form responses that can cause check-in issues on race day.

**One transaction, one record** — A single payment and a single confirmation simplifies bookkeeping and internal reporting for your agency.

**Centralized management** — All registrations made under your transaction are tied together, making it easy to verify who is registered and follow up with athletes who still need to complete their information.

## Coordinating with Event Organizers

Organizers actively welcome assessoria partnerships because agencies bring engaged, committed participants. To make the most of this relationship:

- **Communicate early** — Let the organizer know your agency plans to bring a group. This can lead to reserved tent space, group discount codes, or promotional batch pricing specifically for your athletes.
- **Use the assessoria name field** — When registering athletes, make sure the assessoria name is recorded consistently. Organizers can pull this data after the event to identify agencies they haven't yet connected with and reach out to build ongoing relationships.
- **Ask about partnership perks** — Some organizers offer discount coupons, complimentary entries, or exclusive batch pricing to assessorias that bring larger groups. It never hurts to ask.

<Note>
  Organizers can create discount coupons on Atletis with a fixed value, a percentage, or a complimentary (zero-cost) entry. If the organizer has set up a coupon for your agency, apply the code during checkout to see the discount reflected in the cart total before completing payment.
</Note>
