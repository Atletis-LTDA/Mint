Whether you're setting up your first event or scaling an established race series, the questions below cover the topics organizers ask most often. If you don't find what you're looking for here, reach out to the Atletis support team at [contato@atletis.com.br](mailto:contato@atletis.com.br).

<AccordionGroup>

  <Accordion title="How does Atletis work?">
    Atletis is a complete platform for selling registrations and managing sports events online. As an organizer, you create an event page, configure registration batches (lotes), set up your registration form, and publish. Participants find your event on atletis.com.br or via a direct link, register, and pay — all without you needing a separate website, payment processor, or spreadsheet to track sign-ups.

    The platform handles the full participant journey: registration, payment confirmation, confirmation email delivery, check-in on race day, and financial reporting afterward.
  </Accordion>

  <Accordion title="How do I create my event on Atletis?">
    Creating an event is free to start. Simply sign up for an account at [atletis.com.br](https://www.atletis.com.br) and follow the event creation flow. You'll be guided through the key setup steps: event details, date and location, registration categories, batch pricing, your custom registration form, and visibility settings.

    Once you've filled in the information and published the event, your dedicated event page is live and ready to accept registrations.
  </Accordion>

  <Accordion title="My event doesn't have a website — can I use Atletis?">
    Yes — and this is one of Atletis's most practical features. When you finish setting up your event on the platform, Atletis automatically generates a dedicated event page with a shareable link. That page **is** your event website.

    It displays all the information you've entered: event description, date, location, categories, regulations, and the registration button. You can share the link on social media, WhatsApp, or anywhere else you promote your event. No separate website needed.
  </Accordion>

  <Accordion title="Can I configure automatic batch (lote) transitions?">
    Yes. Inside the event creation area, you can configure when each batch closes and the next one opens. Transitions can be triggered in two ways:

    - **By date** — the batch automatically switches at a date and time you specify.
    - **By quantity** — the batch closes once it reaches the maximum number of registrations you've set for it, and the next batch opens automatically.

    You can also combine both triggers: a batch closes whichever comes first — the date or the quantity limit. This lets you create structured early-bird pricing without having to manually intervene when a batch sells out.
  </Accordion>

  <Accordion title="Do participants receive confirmation emails?">
    Yes. As soon as a participant's payment is confirmed, Atletis automatically sends them a confirmation email with the details of their registration and the event. This applies to both paid and free events.

    For bank-slip (boleto) payments, confirmation is sent once the boleto is processed, which can take up to three business days after payment.
  </Accordion>

  <Accordion title="Should I choose 'public event' or 'private event'?">
    The choice depends on how you want to control access to your event page:

    - **Public event** — your event is listed on atletis.com.br and is discoverable by anyone browsing the platform. This maximizes your event's visibility and is the right choice for most open competitions.
    - **Private event** — your event page is not listed on atletis.com.br. Only people who receive the direct link can access and register. This is useful for invite-only competitions, corporate events, or situations where you want to control exactly who can sign up.

    You set this preference during event creation and can update it at any time before publishing.
  </Accordion>

  <Accordion title="How and when do I receive my payout?">
    Atletis transfers your event revenue on the **next business day after the event takes place**. The payout covers the registration fees collected, net of the platform's processing fee.

    One organizer put it this way: *"The payout lands correctly on the next business day after the event. I've run more than one event with them and I'll run every future one the same way."*
  </Accordion>

  <Accordion title="Can I create discount coupons for my event?">
    Yes. Atletis supports three types of discount coupons, which you can create at any time from the **Coupons** section of your event dashboard:

    - **Fixed value** — a set amount (e.g., R$ 20 off) deducted from the cart total at checkout.
    - **Percentage** — a percentage discount (between 5% and 50%) applied to the cart total.
    - **Complimentary (cortesia)** — a zero-cost registration used for VIPs, partners, sponsors, or athletes you want to invite free of charge.

    For each coupon you can set an optional usage limit and an expiration date, giving you full control over how many times a code can be used and for how long.
  </Accordion>

  <Accordion title="What happens if a participant registers but doesn't show up (no-show)?">
    A registered participant who does not attend the event is treated as a sold registration. No-shows do not trigger an automatic refund — the registration was confirmed and paid, and that slot was reserved.

    Refund eligibility depends entirely on the refund policy you communicate in your event's regulations. Atletis does not manage refunds on your behalf; any refund decisions are between you and the participant, according to the policy you have established for your event.
  </Accordion>

  <Accordion title="Is Atletis secure?">
    Yes. Atletis uses SSL encryption for all data transmitted on the platform. Payment processing is handled through regulated financial infrastructure, ensuring your participants' payment data is protected in accordance with applicable standards.

    Atletis has a clean record on Reclame Aqui, Brazil's leading consumer complaints platform, reflecting a consistent track record of resolving issues and treating both organizers and participants fairly.
  </Accordion>

  <Accordion title="Can I manage multiple events from one account?">
    Yes. A single Atletis login gives you access to all of your events through the **My Events** dashboard. You can switch between events, monitor registrations and financials, create coupons, and manage participant data for each event independently — all from the same account without needing to log in and out.

    This makes Atletis particularly well-suited for professional organizers who run multiple competitions throughout the year.
  </Accordion>

  <Accordion title="What sports can I organize events for on Atletis?">
    Atletis supports more than 20 sports modalities. You can organize events for:

    Canoeing · Cycling · Running · CrossFit · American Football · Gymnastics · Handball · Judo · Martial Arts · Mountaineering · Swimming · Rugby · Skateboarding · Tennis · Triathlon · Surf · Volleyball · Basketball · Beach Tennis · Duathlon · and more.

    If your sport isn't on the list above, select **Others** during event setup or contact the Atletis team — the platform is flexible enough to support virtually any competitive or participatory sports event.
  </Accordion>
