Creating a sports event on Atletis takes just a few minutes. From a single dashboard at [app.atletis.com.br](https://app.atletis.com.br), you can configure every detail of your event — name, date, location, sport type, categories, pricing, and more — then publish it instantly with a dedicated registration page that athletes can find on the Atletis event calendar or through a direct link you share.

Before you start filling in fields, make sure you have the core details of your event ready. Rushing into the form without those decisions made often leads to manual corrections after registration is already open.

## Before you begin: pre-launch checklist

Use this checklist to confirm your event is ready to configure on Atletis:

<CardGroup cols={2}>
  <Card title="Event identity" icon="tag">
    - Official name
    - Sport type / modality
    - Objective and target audience
    - Visual identity and logo
  </Card>
  <Card title="Date & location" icon="map-pin">
    - Event date and start time
    - City, state, and venue address
    - Start/concentration point
    - Any required permits or licenses
  </Card>
  <Card title="Categories & pricing" icon="list">
    - Categories (age group, gender, distance, level)
    - Maximum participants per category
    - Registration fees and batch (lote) rules
  </Card>
  <Card title="Operations" icon="gear">
    - Sponsors and partners
    - Regulations and participation rules
    - Communication channels for athletes
    - Check-in and kit distribution plan
  </Card>
</CardGroup>

<Tip>
  Events with free registration cost nothing to host on Atletis. Paid events are charged a service fee starting at R$ 1.99 per registration — there are no upfront costs.
</Tip>

## Create your event step by step

<Steps>
  <Step title="Access the event creation page">
    Go to [app.atletis.com.br/evento/criar](https://app.atletis.com.br/evento/criar). If you don't have an account yet, you can sign up for free using your Google account, Facebook account, or email address. One account lets you manage multiple events from a single dashboard.
  </Step>

  <Step title="Fill in the basic event details">
    Provide the essential information that appears on your public event page:

    - **Event name** — Use the official name your athletes will recognize. Keep it clear and searchable.
    - **Sport category** — Select the modality that best describes your event (running, cycling, volleyball, CrossFit, martial arts, and many more).
    - **Date, start time, and end time** — Confirm the exact date and schedule before publishing.
    - **Location** — Enter the city, state, venue name, and address. You can also mark the location on the integrated map so athletes can find it easily.
    - **Format** — Indicate whether the event is in-person, online, or hybrid.

    Click **Proceed** (Prosseguir) when you're done with this section.
  </Step>

  <Step title="Write your event description">
    Your description is often the first thing a potential participant reads. A strong description should explain:

    - What the event is and who it is for
    - What makes this edition special (new route, new category, bigger prizes, etc.)
    - Any important requirements participants should know upfront

    <Tip>
      Aim for 2–4 short paragraphs. Athletes scan quickly — lead with the most compelling information. Avoid copying dense regulatory text into the description; link to your full regulations separately.
    </Tip>
  </Step>

  <Step title="Upload your cover image and logo">
    Your cover image appears at the top of your event page and in the Atletis event calendar listing. Use a high-quality image that represents your event — an action photo from a previous edition, a course panorama, or professional artwork works well.

    <Tip>
      Use a wide-format image (landscape orientation) for the cover. Avoid images with too much small text, since the image is also shown as a thumbnail in search results. Upload your event logo separately so it appears alongside the event name.
    </Tip>
  </Step>

  <Step title="Add contact information">
    Enter the name and WhatsApp number of the organizer or support contact. This is what athletes see when they need help before or after registering. Make sure the number is monitored during the registration period.
  </Step>

  <Step title="Configure categories and pricing">
    After the basic details, you'll set up the registration structure for your event:

    - **Create categories** — Add categories by age group, gender, distance, experience level, or any combination. Each category can have its own description, price, and participant limit. See [Set Up Registration Categories](/organizers/categories) for a detailed walkthrough.
    - **Set batch (lote) rules** — Configure automatic price increases triggered by a date or by the number of registrations sold. Early-bird pricing encourages athletes to register sooner.
    - **Choose payment methods** — Atletis accepts Pix, credit card (including installments), and bank slip (boleto). You can choose whether service fees are absorbed by the organizer or passed on to the participant.

    <Note>
      Complimentary (cortesia) registrations and discount coupons are available on paid events. Coupons can be scoped to specific groups such as sponsors, training groups, or influencers.
    </Note>
  </Step>

  <Step title="Set visibility: public or private">
    Choose whether your event is **public** or **private**:

    - **Public** — Your event is listed in the Atletis event calendar at [atletis.com.br/eventos](https://www.atletis.com.br/eventos) and is discoverable by any athlete browsing the platform.
    - **Private** — Your event is not listed in the calendar. Only people who have the direct link can access the registration page. This is useful for internal corporate events, club competitions, or invite-only races.
  </Step>

  <Step title="Review and publish">
    Before publishing, review your event page as if you were an athlete seeing it for the first time:

    - Is the event name, date, and location clearly visible?
    - Are the categories and prices easy to understand?
    - Is there enough information to make a registration decision?
    - Is the contact information correct?

    When everything looks right, click **Publish Event** (Publicar evento) in the Summary tab. Your event goes live immediately. For public events, it appears in the Atletis calendar right away. You'll receive a notification confirming your page is active.

    <Note>
      After publishing, you can still edit event details, add categories, update descriptions, and adjust batch rules at any time from your organizer dashboard.
    </Note>
  </Step>
</Steps>

## After publishing

Once your event is live, Atletis gives you a shareable URL — your event's unique direct link — that you can post on social media, WhatsApp groups, and anywhere else you promote your event. Your dashboard shows registrations, payment status, and revenue in real time, so you always know exactly where your event stands.
