When athletes visit your event page, they should see **your** brand — your logo, your colors, your identity — not a generic third-party platform. Atletis white-label technology gives large organizers a fully branded digital home for their event while the entire Atletis infrastructure operates invisibly in the background. You get the credibility of a professional, purpose-built platform without writing a single line of code or managing your own servers.

## What "White Label" Means on Atletis

A white-label solution is a platform built by someone else that you can present entirely as your own. On Atletis, that means your event gets a dedicated page carrying your visual identity — logo, color palette, and overall look — while Atletis handles registration management, payment processing, batch (lote) control, participant data, and reporting behind the scenes.

You never lose ownership of your brand. Participants interact with **your event page**. The organizational muscle powering it just happens to be Atletis.

## Who Is It For?

White-label is designed for organizers who have outgrown a basic event listing and need a professional presence that matches the scale of their events:

- **Large-scale events** with hundreds or thousands of participants
- **Recurring event series** that need consistent brand recognition across multiple editions
- **Professional organizing companies** that manage events as a core business and need to project credibility to sponsors, participants, and partners

If you organize smaller or one-off events, Atletis's standard paid plan already gives you a dedicated event page and full feature access — white-label is the upgrade layer on top of that.

## What You Can Customize

Your white-label event page reflects your brand from top to bottom:

- **Logo** — displayed prominently so participants always know whose event they're registering for
- **Visual identity** — colors and design elements aligned with your brand guidelines
- **Custom domain or subdomain** — your event lives at a web address that belongs to your brand, not a generic atletis.com.br URL

## Benefits for Your Brand and Your Participants

Running your event on a white-label platform pays dividends on multiple fronts:

**Credibility** — A polished, branded registration page signals to participants, sponsors, and partners that your event is professionally run. It reinforces that this is a serious competition, not a side project.

**Brand recognition** — Every touchpoint in the participant journey — the event page, confirmation email, check-in experience, and results page — carries your brand. Over time, athletes associate quality and reliability directly with your name.

**Professional participant experience** — Participants get a seamless, end-to-end digital journey: discovering your event, registering online, receiving confirmation, accessing route maps and regulations, and viewing their results — all within a consistent branded environment.

**Operational efficiency** — You focus on the strategic work of building a great event. Atletis handles the infrastructure.

3. **Discuss your requirements** — a specialist will reach out to understand your brand guidelines, technical needs, and timeline.
