Registration categories are the backbone of your event's participant structure. They determine which registration option each athlete chooses, how the check-in list is organized, how kits are distributed, and how results are classified. Getting your categories right before you open registrations saves you from manual corrections and confusion on race day.

Atletis lets you create as many categories as your event needs, each with its own name, description, price, and participant limit. Categories appear clearly in the registration flow so athletes can identify the right option for their profile.

---

## What categories are for

A category in Atletis represents a distinct group of participants who share a common characteristic — and who will register, compete, or participate together under that label.

Common ways to define categories include:

<CardGroup cols={2}>
  <Card title="By distance or course" icon="route">
    Separate registration options for different race distances or routes — for example, 5 km, 10 km, and 21 km in a road race; short course vs. long course in a triathlon.
  </Card>
  <Card title="By age group" icon="cake-candles">
    Groups defined by minimum and maximum age — for example, Under 18, 18–29, 30–39, 40–49, 50+. Age group categories are common in running, cycling, swimming, and team sports.
  </Card>
  <Card title="By gender" icon="venus-mars">
    Separate categories for male and female participants, or open/mixed categories for events where gender is not a defining classification criterion.
  </Card>
  <Card title="By experience or level" icon="signal">
    Categories such as Beginner, Intermediate, and Advanced — or sport-specific labels like Amateur, Age Group, and Elite — help athletes self-select the right competitive tier.
  </Card>
  <Card title="By team or group" icon="people-group">
    For team sports or relay events, categories can represent team size or format — e.g., Solo, Duo, Team of 4. Each registration then covers one participant unit within that structure.
  </Card>
  <Card title="By modality" icon="dumbbell">
    For multi-sport events or tournaments with distinct disciplines, categories can separate participants by the specific activity they're entering.
  </Card>
</CardGroup>

<Note>
  You can combine these dimensions in a single event. For example, a running event might have categories for "Male 30–39 / 10 km", "Female 40–49 / 10 km", "Male Open / 5 km", and so on. Each combination is its own category in Atletis.
</Note>

## Create categories in your dashboard

<Steps>
  <Step title="Open the Categories section">
    In your organizer dashboard, open the event you want to configure and navigate to the **Categories** (Categorias) section. This is where all registration categories for your event are created and managed.
  </Step>

  <Step title="Add a new category">
    Click **Add category** (Adicionar categoria). A form opens for you to define the details of this category.
  </Step>

  <Step title="Name the category">
    Give the category a clear, descriptive name that athletes will immediately understand. Examples:

    - `Male — 10 km`
    - `Female — 5 km`
    - `Open Mixed — 21 km`
    - `Beginner — Individual`
    - `Elite — Team of 4`

    The name appears on the registration page, in your participant list, on the check-in app, and in any results data you export.
  </Step>

  <Step title="Write a category description (optional but recommended)">
    Add a short description explaining who this category is for, any eligibility rules, and what is included. For example:

    > *For male participants aged 30–39 running the 10 km route. Starting wave: Wave B at 07:30. Includes race kit with shirt and medal.*

    A good description reduces the number of athletes who choose the wrong category and then contact you to switch.
  </Step>

  <Step title="Set the participant limit">
    Enter the maximum number of participants allowed in this category. When the limit is reached, the category automatically closes and no further registrations are accepted for it.

    <Tip>
      Set limits based on your logistical capacity — wave size, kit inventory, timing system slots, or field/court size. It's better to set a realistic limit from the start than to manually close a category later.
    </Tip>
  </Step>

  <Step title="Configure pricing and batches">
    Set the registration price for this category and configure any batch (lote) rules. You can:

    - Set an initial (early-bird) price for the first batch
    - Define when the batch changes — by a specific date or when a set number of registrations is reached
    - Set the price for subsequent batches

    Batch pricing is managed per category, so different categories can have different price tiers and different deadlines.

    <Note>
      Complimentary (cortesia) registrations can also be assigned to specific categories. This is useful for inviting sponsors, VIPs, or press to participate at no charge.
    </Note>
  </Step>

  <Step title="Save and repeat">
    Save the category and repeat the process for each additional category your event requires. You can reorder, edit, or delete categories at any time before and after publishing — as long as no registrations have been confirmed for a category you want to delete.
  </Step>
</Steps>

## How categories appear during registration

When an athlete visits your event page and clicks to register, they first see the list of available categories. For each category, Atletis displays:

- The category name
- The description (if you added one)
- The current price and the deadline for the current batch
- The number of spots remaining (once the limit is nearly reached)

The athlete selects their category and proceeds to fill in the registration form and complete payment. The selected category is recorded as part of their registration record.

<Warning>
  If a category's description or eligibility criteria are unclear, athletes may register in the wrong category. This creates extra work for your team — manual transfers, kit re-assignments, and timing corrections. Invest a few extra minutes in writing clear category descriptions before you open registrations.
</Warning>

## How category data is used after registration

Categories feed directly into the operational side of your event:

- **Participant list** — Your exported participant list is sortable and filterable by category. This is the foundation for check-in lists, kit distribution manifests, and start wave assignments.
- **Check-in app** — The Atletis check-in app lets your team validate participants on event day by name, CPF, or QR code, with the category clearly visible for each registrant. You can filter the check-in view by category to manage separate check-in queues.
- **Kit distribution** — If different categories receive different kit items (different shirt colors, different medal types, different race bags), the category label on each participant's record guides your team at the distribution table.
- **Results** — Category data organizes your post-event results. Participants are ranked within their own category, and you can generate category-level reports from your dashboard.
