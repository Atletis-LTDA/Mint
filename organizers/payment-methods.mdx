Atletis offers participants three payment methods — Pix, credit card, and boleto bancário (bank slip) — all processed inside a single, transparent checkout. Participants complete their purchase without being redirected to a third-party site, which reduces friction and increases conversion. Every transaction is protected by an SSL certificate and the regulated payment protections required by Brazilian financial rules.

## Payment method comparison

| | Pix | Credit card | Boleto bancário |
|---|-----|-------------|-----------------|
| **Confirmation speed** | Seconds | Seconds | Up to 3 business days |
| **Installments** | Not available | Available | Not available |
| **Availability window** | Until event closes | Until event closes | Up to **72 hours before** the event |
| **Organizer receives** | Amount after fees | Full amount upfront | Amount after fees, once confirmed |
| **Participant experience** | Scan QR code or copy-paste key | Enter card details | Download and pay slip at bank or app |

## How each method works

### Pix

Pix is Brazil's instant payment system. When a participant selects Pix at checkout, they receive a QR code and a copy-paste key. Payment is confirmed in **seconds**, and the registration status updates immediately. Because confirmation is instant, Pix is available right up until registrations close.

### Credit card

Participants can pay by credit card in a **single payment or in installments**, depending on the options you configure for your event. When a participant pays by credit card, the organizer receives the **full registration amount upfront** — Atletis handles any installment arrangement on the back end. Card payments are confirmed in seconds, just like Pix.

### Boleto bancário

A boleto is a printed or digital bank slip that participants pay at a bank branch, lottery house, or through their banking app. Because boleto compensation takes up to **3 business days**, this option is only available up to **72 hours before the event start time**. Any registration purchased by boleto within that 72-hour window may not be confirmed before the event — participants who choose boleto close to the deadline should be aware of this timing.

<Note>
  If a participant pays a boleto but their registration still shows **"pending"** after 3 business days, ask them to contact the Atletis support team at [contato@atletis.com.br](mailto:contato@atletis.com.br). Boleto confirmation delays beyond 3 days are uncommon but do occur.
</Note>

## Who pays the platform fees

Atletis charges a per-registration fee on paid events:

- **R$ 1.99** per registration with a value up to R$ 35
- **7.99%** of the registration value for registrations above R$ 35

As the organizer, **you decide** whether to absorb this fee or pass it on to the participant as a service/convenience fee (taxa de serviço or taxa de conveniência). Both options are available when you configure your event:

- **Absorb the fee** — participants pay the face value you set. The fee comes out of your payout. This can be a compelling selling point, especially for smaller local events where every real counts.
- **Pass the fee to participants** — the checkout displays the base registration price plus the service fee. Participants see the total before confirming. This approach is common and widely accepted in paid sports events.

<Tip>
  For events where your audience is accustomed to paying a convenience fee — larger races, multi-day competitions, professional leagues — passing the fee to participants is standard practice and unlikely to deter sign-ups. For smaller community events, absorbing the fee can increase perceived value and help fill the remaining spots.
</Tip>

## Payout timing

Revenue from registrations is transferred to your bank account on the **next business day after the event takes place**. You do not need to request a manual transfer — payouts are processed automatically after your event date.

<Note>
  Payouts are made after the event date, not after each individual registration. Plan your event budget with this timing in mind, particularly for expenses that need to be paid before the event day.
</Note>

## Checkout experience and security

The entire checkout — from selecting a registration category to confirming payment — takes place on the Atletis platform. Participants are never redirected to an external payment gateway. This **transparent checkout** approach:

- Keeps participants within a familiar, trusted environment
- Gives you real-time sale notifications for every completed purchase
- Reduces cart abandonment caused by unexpected redirects or unfamiliar payment pages

All transactions are encrypted with an SSL certificate, and Atletis complies with all Brazilian regulations for payment processing and data privacy.
