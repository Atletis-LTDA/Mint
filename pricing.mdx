Atletis uses a straightforward pay-per-registration model: you pay nothing to create an event, nothing for free-admission events, and a small per-registration fee only when a paid registration is successfully completed. There is no monthly subscription, no annual contract, and no setup fee. You only pay when you sell.

## Free Events — R$ 0

The Free Events plan is designed for community competitions, club championships, or any event where participation is free. There are no hidden costs — Atletis covers the full platform so you can focus on your event.

**What's included:**

- Dedicated event page with your own direct link
- Online organizer dashboard
- Full participant management
- Check-in app for race day
- Personalized registration PDF for each participant

<Note>
  Even for free events, participants should still register through your Atletis page. This allows you to know exactly how many people will attend and manage logistics accurately.
</Note>

## Paid Events — From R$ 1.99 per Registration

For events that charge a registration fee, Atletis applies a transparent per-registration fee — and only when a registration is successfully sold.

### Fee Structure

| Registration Price | Atletis Fee |
|---|---|
| Up to R$ 35.00 | **R$ 1.99 flat** per registration |
| Above R$ 35.00 | **7.99%** of the registration price |

<Note>
  Fees apply **only to sold registrations**. If a registration is not completed or not paid, no fee is charged. There is no monthly fee, no minimum volume, and no setup cost.
</Note>

**What's included, on top of everything in Free Events:**

- Pix, credit card, and boleto bancário payment acceptance
- Installment payments — you receive the full amount upfront
- Courtesy (complimentary) registrations for guests or sponsors
- Discount coupons for sponsors, running clubs, influencers, and more
- Batch (lote) control with automatic date- or volume-triggered transitions
- Custom registration form per category
- Financial reports and registration analytics
- Public participant list
- CPF-unique registration control (prevents duplicate sign-ups per person)
- Listing on the Atletis events calendar

### Who Pays the Fee?

You decide. Atletis gives you full flexibility:

<CardGroup cols={2}>
  <Card title="Pass it to the participant" icon="arrow-right-arrow-left">
    Add the fee as a service or convenience charge on top of the registration price. This is common for larger events where participants expect a convenience fee.
  </Card>
  <Card title="Absorb it yourself" icon="hand-holding-dollar">
    Keep the registration price clean and cover the fee from your revenue. This can be an effective strategy for smaller events to increase perceived value and attract more sign-ups.
  </Card>
</CardGroup>

<Tip>
  Combining absorbed fees with well-planned lote transitions and discount coupons can increase registrations without reducing your overall margin.
</Tip>

## Frequently Asked Questions

**Is there a minimum number of registrations required?**
No. You can use Atletis for an event of any size — even a small local competition with a handful of participants.

**When are fees charged?**
Fees are applied per approved, paid registration. You are never charged for unpaid or incomplete registrations.

**Can I change my pricing or batches after publishing?**
Yes. You can edit pricing and batch configurations in your organizer dashboard at any time before the batch or event closes.

**Are installment payments available?**
Yes, for paid events. Participants can pay in installments by credit card, and you receive the full registration amount upfront.
