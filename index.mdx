## Get Up and Running in 4 Steps

<Steps>
  <Step title="Create your free account">
    Go to [app.atletis.com.br/evento/criar](https://app.atletis.com.br/evento/criar) and sign up. Your account is free and gives you access to the full organizer dashboard.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Configure your event">
    Add your event name, date, location, sport modality, and description. Atletis generates a dedicated public page instantly.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Set up categories and pricing">
    Define registration categories, pricing tiers (lotes), and accepted payment methods. Configure automatic batch transitions so prices increase as the event fills up.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Publish and share">
    Hit **Publish** and share your event link. Participants can register and pay directly on your Atletis page.
  </Step>
</Steps>

<Note>
  Events with free registrations cost nothing to create or manage on Atletis. Platform fees only apply when participants pay for registrations.
</Note>
